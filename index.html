Level 017 · Why Architecture?

An organisation has to keep going.

It has to pay for the work already done,

keep the promises already made,

find the means for work still to come.

And the way it keeps going makes things possible

for people it will never meet.

The architecture is not the building.

It is the relationships through which people judge, learn and change course.

Level 019 · Author’s Preface

Forty years in banking.

What happens to a decision after the people who took it have moved on?

Level 042 · Offices, Records and Answerability

Level 043 · What the Law Asks of Companies and Directors

A company owes obligations in its own name.

Directors owe their duties to the company.

Companies Act 2006 · s.172(1) · having regard to
  1. the likely consequences of any decision in the long term
  2. the interests of the company’s employees
  3. relationships with suppliers, customers and others
  4. the impact on the community and the environment
  5. a reputation for high standards of business conduct
  6. acting fairly as between members

Without records that can be followed and questioned,

every change of personnel makes the past harder to know.

Level 129 · Recognising Responsibility

The definition the whole descent has been building towards.

A responsible organisation
  1. pursues its purposes and sustains its commercial viability
  2. meets its legal obligations
  3. takes moral responsibility for how its activity affects others
  4. builds the relationships, authority, knowledge and resources through which people can judge, question, learn and respond
  5. and gives these commitments practical meaning through its culture and collective conduct

Each generation receives something it did not create,

finds out more of what it means, and adds to what others will inherit.
